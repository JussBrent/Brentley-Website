Notify+

Faster than the drop.

An asyncio inventory monitoring platform that alerts a paid community the moment limited sneakers, trading cards and streetwear restock.

What it achieved

  • $2K+ MRR from a paid community of 200+ members
  • Alerts land in 3 to 4 seconds from stock change to Discord notification
  • Stays up through high-traffic drops when demand peaks

How I built it

  • Concurrent site polling with Python asyncio and aiohttp
  • Per-proxy health tracking, exponential backoff and automatic dead-proxy removal across 50+ proxies
  • Queued alert delivery with request pacing and automatic retries to handle rate limits
  • Persistent state storage so restarts never resend or miss alerts
