Sentinel Utilities

Three utilities. One map. Zero collisions.

A full-stack geospatial platform that finds location and scheduling overlaps across infrastructure projects from three utility providers.

What it achieved

  • Analyzed 272 projects from public filings
  • Flagged 61 potential overlaps within 25 miles
  • Matched all 6 organizer-confirmed overlaps

How I built it

  • Data-processing pipeline in Python to parse and normalize public utility filings
  • PostgreSQL location-based queries to compare projects by distance and schedule
  • Interactive map of projects and overlapping construction schedules with Leaflet and Next.js
